# 24CYS336 - Blockchain-Technology 

## Lab 5: Blockchain Decision Tree 

### Objectives
- To analyze all the **Smart India Hackathon (SIH) 2025 Problem Statements - Blockchain & Cyber Security** using a **Blockchain Decision Tree**.
- To understand whether Blockchain is a suitable solution for the given problem statements.
- To develop **critical thinking** in identifying domains where Blockchain can provide value such as transparency, immutability, decentralization, and trust.
- To prepare a **structured documentation report** showcasing the decision-making process.

### Background
The **Blockchain Decision Tree** is a systematic approach used to decide if a problem really requires Blockchain as a solution.  
Many projects and ideas adopt Blockchain without evaluating its actual necessity, leading to inefficiency.  
By applying the decision tree to each SIH 2025 Problem Statement, you will:
- Explore if Blockchain adds real value.
- Distinguish between problems solvable by traditional databases and those requiring Blockchain.
- Justify the **YES/NO** decision at each step of the decision tree.

Your documentation should include:
1. **Problem Statement ID & Title** (from SIH 2025 portal).
2. **Decision Tree Analysis** – a step-by-step evaluation.
3. **Final Verdict** – Blockchain required or not (with reasoning).
4. **Summary Table** for quick reference.
