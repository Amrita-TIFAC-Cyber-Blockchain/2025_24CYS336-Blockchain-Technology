### Problem Statement
Despite the advancements in digital healthcare, the current systems have a hard time delivering a safe and patient-centric environment. Patients have little visibility and control over who can access their medical records, and centralized data is vulnerable to unauthorized access. AI models utilized for diagnosis are open to attacks that can affect their accuracy, potentially threatening lives. On top of that, the blockchain-based solutions suffer from high and unstable transaction fees which limits their practical use. Without transparent audit trails or verifiable AI decisions, there is still a major challenge in trust and accountability when it comes to healthcare technology nowadays.

### Literature Survey 
[1] Alabdulatif et al. (2023)
Title: Leveraging Artificial Intelligence in Blockchain-Based E-Health for Safer Decision Making
Link: https://doi.org/10.3390/app13021035
This paper proposes a secure healthcare framework that combines AI with blockchain to support safe and trustworthy medical decision-making. It highlights how AI-driven predictions can be protected from tampering by recording them on a blockchain, improving integrity and auditability. The study also discusses access control mechanisms to ensure only authorized users can retrieve or modify Electronic Health Records (EHRs).

[2] Mandarino et al. (2024)
Title: A Blockchain-Based Electronic Health Record (EHR) System for Edge Computing Enhancing Security and Cost Efficiency
Link: https://doi.org/10.3390/computers13060132
This work introduces a blockchain-based EHR system integrated with edge computing to address the challenges of security, latency, and high operational costs. By shifting computation closer to the data source (i.e., edge devices), the system reduces reliance on centralized servers and minimizes blockchain transaction costs, while still preserving data confidentiality and integrity.
