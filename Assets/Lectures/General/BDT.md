# 24CYS336 - Blockchain-Technology 

## Blockchain Decision Tree
BDT helps an individual or organization analyze, interpret their usecase and finally decide the suitability of their usecases or application for Blockchain Technology. \newline
There are many Decision Trees available that can be of help:
- CompTIA's When is Blockchain or other database a right choice?
- IEEE Decision Tree
- Government of India - NITI Aayog's Decision Tree

### NITI Aayog's Decision Tree
