# 24CYS336 - Blockchain-Technology 

## Characteristics of Blockchain
- Immutable – permanent and tamper-proof
- Decentralized & Distributed – local copies synced across nodes
- Consensus Driven – trust and decision-making via consensus
- Transparent – open and public (“Single Source of Truth”)

#### Immutability
- Append-only ledger – no modification or deletion.
- Example: Ancient stone inscriptions → tamper-evident.

#### Decentralization & Distribution
- Data stored across all nodes.
- Computation is decentralized.
- Eliminates single point of failure.

#### Consensus Driven
- Distributed nodes establish trust through consensus mechanisms.
- Consensus ensures validity of transactions and blocks.

#### Transparency
- Blockchain is open and public.
- All transactions are visible and verifiable.
- Acts as a “Single Source of Truth”.
