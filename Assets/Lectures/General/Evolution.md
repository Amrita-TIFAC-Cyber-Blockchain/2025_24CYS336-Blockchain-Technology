# 24CYS336 - Blockchain-Technology 

## Evolution of Blockchain Technology

- 1976–77 – **Digital signatures** theorized (Diffie & Hellman); RSA algorithm (Rivest, Shamir, Adleman).
- 1979 – Ralph Merkle patents the **Merkle Tree**.
- 1989 – [Leslie Lamport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leslie_Lamport) introduces the Paxos Consensus Algorithm.
- 1991 – First mention of a cryptographically secured chain of blocks (Haber & Stornetta).
- 1993 – DigiCash demo by David Chaum.
- 1993 – **SHA-0** published by NIST.
- 1997 – **Nick Szab**o introduces **Smart Contracts**; Adam Back proposes _Hashcash_.
- 1998 – Szabo works on bit gold; Wei Dai creates _b-money_.
- 1999 – Jakobsson & Juels publish “Proofs of Work and Bread Pudding Protocols.”
- 2002 – Adam Back publishes on Hashcash as a DoS countermeasure.
- 2004 – Hal Finney announces **Reusable Proof of Work (RPoW)**.
- 2008 – **Satoshi Nakamoto** releases _Bitcoin_ Whitepaper.
- 2010 – First Bitcoin exchange and the famous [Bitcoin Pizza Transaction](https://www.blockchain.com/explorer/transactions/btc/a1075db55d416d3ca199f55b6084e2115b9345e16c5cf302fc80e9d5fbf5d48d).
- 2013 – **Vitalik Buterin** releases _Ethereum_ Whitepaper.
- 2015 – Ethereum Blockchain launches; Linux Foundation starts Hyperledger Project.
- 2016 – DAO Attack → split into Ethereum & Ethereum Classic.
- 2017 – Lightning Network Protocol 1.0 released.
- 2018 – Baidu launches **Blockchain as a Service (BaaS)**.
- 2019 – Amazon Web Services announces Managed Blockchain Service.
- 2020 – Ethereum 2.0 Beacon Chain launches—kickstarting PoW-to-PoS transition.
- 2021 – El Salvador adopts Bitcoin as legal tender; Government of India launches **National Blockchain Project**.
- 2022 – Ethereum completes **The Merge (PoW → PoS)**; India launched pilot programs for the **Digital Rupee (CBDC)** — both wholesale (e₹-W) and retail (e₹-R).
