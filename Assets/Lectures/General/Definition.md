## Definition - Blockchain

### IBM
IBM defines Blockchain as a shared, immutable ledger that facilitates the process of recording transactions and tracking assets in a business network. An asset can be tangible like a car, house, land or intangible like intellectual property, patents, copyrights even brands

### What is Blockchain Technology?
**Blockchain Ledger** is a data structure similar to a linked list where every block is a data container linked with each other cryptographically. <br/>

## Definition - Distributed Ledger Technology
A **DLT** is that enables the operation and use of distributed ledgers. A distributed ledger is designed to be tamper-resistant, append-only and immutable containing confirmed and validated transactions. Blockchain is one form of DLT, others include Tangle and Hashgraph.

### Alex Tapscott and Don Tapscott
A distributed ledger is a database existing in many places with many people using it. A blockchain is a specific type of distributed ledger, just as a square is a specific type of rectangle. The blockchain is public, it's open-source code, it's a protocol, not a product. Anyone can view it at any time because it's located on the network, not inside a single institution.
