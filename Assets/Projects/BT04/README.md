### Problem Statement
A notary is a legal authority who verifies and authenticates documents, ensuring that agreements, contracts, or certificates are valid and willingly signed. Traditional notary processes involve physical paperwork, manual verification, and are often time-consuming and prone to forgery. This project proposes a blockchain-based digital notary system that leverages the immutability, transparency, and timestamping capabilities of blockchain technology to automate and secure document notarization.

-----
### Literature Survey 

1. **Blockchain in Digital Notary Services - Goncharova.A (2024)** <br/>
The study highlights blockchain’s potential to modernize traditional notarial processes through decentralization, transparency, and data integrity. It emphasizes that blockchain can automate verification, prevent forgery, and reduce bureaucratic delays in document authentication. However, challenges such as privacy concerns, legal adaptation, and technical risks must be addressed for effective implementation. The integration of smart contracts and digital signatures further strengthens trust and efficiency in digital notarization systems.

2. **A Framework for Sharing Student-Owned Educational Data on Public Blockchain Network - Mustafa Tanriverdi (2024)** <br/>
This artical is introduced, allowing students to own and manage their data through smart contracts on the public Ethereum network.Educational institutionscan access and update data via Learning Management Systems (LMS), ensuring a decentralized, student- controlled data system. This framework enables secure, shared access to educational data across institutions.

3. **Blockchain based document verification system - Mainali,Rathore,Maheshwari,Sharma (2023)** <br/>
This article propose a blockchain-based solution for securely storing and verifying educational certificates. Documents are converted into a one-way hash and stored on the blockchain. Employers can verify authenticity by comparing the hash with the blockchain record. If altered, the hashes won’t match, ensuring tamper-proof credential validation.
